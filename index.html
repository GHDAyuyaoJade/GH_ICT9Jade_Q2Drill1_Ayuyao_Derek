Badminton 101 🏸

Basic Rules

  • Serve Diagonally
  • Serve Below the Ribs
  • Win at 21 Points
  • Shuttlecock must fall within boundaries

Badminton Equipment

  • Badminton Racket
  • Plastic/Feathered Shuttlecock
  • Badminton Net
  • Court

Techniques

  • Attack the Opposite Side of the Opponent
  • If the opponent is far, gently hit the shuttlecock
  • If the opponent is near, smash the shuttlecock to the back
  • Practice "split-step"
